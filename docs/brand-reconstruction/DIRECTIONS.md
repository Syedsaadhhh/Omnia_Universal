# Three identity and website directions

Unapproved alternatives, 2026-10-16. Read RESEARCH.md first. These are original concept constructions for review, not final logos or legally cleared assets. The separately generated raster mood board is interpretive; editable SVG geometry and actual browser screenshots are the reproducible studies. Wordmarks explore openly licensed type, not a newly drawn proprietary alphabet.

Open [the review index](concepts/index.html), [identity applications](concepts/identity.html) and [homepage/mobile wireframes](concepts/wireframes.html). Serve locally using the instructions in README.md; no application deployment is needed.

## A — Open Register

**Thesis:** an open question becomes an inspectable record, then something worth building. The impression should be serious, approachable research with engineering consequences—not an established academic institution.

**Symbol:** two asymmetric open orthogonal frames, offset across a gap. The gap expresses an unresolved question; the shared alignment suggests a reusable record. No shield, enclosure of authority, O/M initials or security assertion. This is a proposed interpretation, not a standardized scientific notation. Risk: bracket/frame geometry is common in developer identities; a similarity review and optical refinement are necessary.

**Wordmark/type:** full-name title case in Schibsted Grotesk at weight 600; upright Literata display for the website and reading surfaces. The raster sheet also tests a serif wordmark treatment; the editable study tests the sans wordmark for clearer separation from paper titles. Do not split SEC or abbreviate the organization to Omnia Labs. Avoid microtype and italic-as-decoration. Current vectors are typeset outlines; final spacing is not approved.

**Color:** tinted pine #101F1D, pale mineral #EAF1EB, sage #B3D4A0. Dark primary identity with pale reading applications. On light: text #18362C, muted #405B4E, accent #315A42. On dark: text #EAF1EB, muted #A5BEB6, accent sage. Contrast pairs are measured separately; sage is a surface/accent on dark, not small body text on pale mineral.

**Geometry/graphics:** asymmetric openings and measured gaps, square editorial fields, rules only where they separate records. Sparse, consistent 1.6px line icons for actions; process numbering only for the actual sequence. Research diagrams must explain a real mechanism, never impersonate live telemetry.

**Homepage:** asymmetrical 8/4 composition; large left statement and two audience actions; right-hand intended eight-step method; inspectable historical work; research standards; collaboration and organization paths; founder and dated evidence. Desktop navigation keeps the public task names, not cryptic laboratory categories.

**Mobile:** full name plus Menu; headline, purpose, stage and actions precede the method. At 320px, actions stack and the eight steps become one readable column. Work becomes a list. No clipped desktop diagram or horizontal process rail. Article surfaces should use the pale version for sustained reading, with a dark preference available only if justified by testing.

**Motion:** currently static except native navigation and scrolling. Later: a short, already-visible state transition for opening the menu; no hero entrance that hides content, scroll choreography or canvas. Reduced motion preserves immediate feedback.

**Recruitment / customer fit:** evidence and thoughtful open questions appeal to serious collaborators; explicit problem intake and feasibility language make commercial relevance visible without promising a delivery team. Main risk: editorial restraint can understate practical engineering; the method and source-linked artifacts must remain concrete.

**Applications:** one-color SVG; horizontal full-name lockup; stacked name with symbol; compact symbol at actual 16/24/32px; pale/dark uses; repository, paper and endorsement templates on the identity sheet. Keep at least an 8/56-symbol-width clear space in this draft; exact construction, minimum print size and favicon optical simplification remain unapproved.

**Tradeoffs:** two font families cost more than one; use next/font/local and a small approved subset in implementation, with fallback metrics tested. Dense long-form serif text needs comfortable size/measure. Open-frame marks can look like generic code brackets; do not finalize without comparison and small-size review.

## B — Working Assembly

**Thesis:** complementary disciplines make a working joint. The impression is a capable, tangible engineering/product studio that investigates before it builds.

**Symbol:** two broad offset planes meeting across a deliberate step. The assembly is useful as a repeatable join in diagrams and document layouts. It is not a forward arrow, shield or arbitrary letter monogram. Risk: interlocking geometric symbols and paired slanted planes are crowded territory; screen against industrial, software and logistics marks.

**Wordmark/type:** full-name uppercase Barlow Condensed Semibold, no SEC emphasis; Barlow regular/semibold body. Narrow display creates strong hierarchy without a technology-font costume. Use ordinary width body type for details and long reading. The condensed face is not used for policies or small navigation.

**Color:** chalk #F4F1E8, warm charcoal #292820, fired oxide #A64022. Light-first homepage, charcoal document/brand applications with pale oxide #ECA27D. Body secondary #635B4C on chalk; #C4BDA8 on charcoal. Rust is not used as low-contrast text over dark charcoal.

**Geometry/graphics:** joined planes, rectangular layout fields and purposeful process diagrams. No industrial hazard stripes, blueprint overlay without measurements, wrench imagery or fake factory case study. Action icons share the same line weight as A; system character comes from proportions and composition, not many bespoke UI icons.

**Homepage:** large utilitarian statement on the left; purpose/stage/actions on the right; full-width numbered eight-step sequence; research approach; strong horizontal work rows; two engagement paths; founder and progress evidence. This direction makes the delivery process easiest to scan.

**Mobile:** title and stage before the process; numbered sequence becomes two columns at 390px, readable labels at 320px; work rows become vertical. Full name remains visible. Navigation uses a native expandable menu in the study, with production implementation retaining the tested React behavior.

**Motion:** direct, quick state changes. An approved process diagram could highlight a chosen step on focus, but information is always present and no animation implies a real project stage. No sliding machinery, sound or gratuitous parallax.

**Recruitment / customer fit:** strongest for problem owners needing a clear engagement structure; concrete artifacts reassure contributors that research leads somewhere useful. Risk: the studio character can overstate current capacity or imply fabrication/industrial expertise. The formation statement and feasibility gate are mandatory.

**Applications:** monochrome and reversed symbol; horizontal outlined wordmark; vertical/compact lockups; 16/24/32px tests; light proposal/document use and dark software/social use. Broad forms survive small sizes better than detailed shield geometry, but the central step needs optical testing.

**Tradeoffs:** expressive condensed headings can reduce word-shape recognition when overused; keep descriptive text mixed case. A very tall billboard must not push the stage/actions below the first phone screen. This is the strongest alternative if the owner wants practical product engineering to lead the first impression.

## C — Common Ground

**Thesis:** people with different expertise converge on a shared, testable problem. The impression is an open, ambitious research initiative inviting serious participation.

**Symbol:** three open paths turn toward a common baseline. The editable study uses nested open curves; the raster mood board also explores arch intersections as an alternative within the same concept. Neither is final. Avoid treating the motif as a network protocol or measured data. Risk: curves can evoke connectivity/wireless marks; the arch variant can evoke bridges/infrastructure companies. Both need deliberate similarity screening.

**Wordmark/type:** title-case Commissioner, moderate weight with open counters; one family across display, navigation, body and records. More human and outward-facing than A or B. No monospaced marketing copy. Preserve the full name at small sizes instead of a tiny descriptor line.

**Color:** deep blue #101D4C, cobalt #1735A6, pale limestone #F2F3E3, yellow #EAD269. Cobalt hero and pale research surfaces; deep-blue longer framing areas. Muted #D2DCFF on cobalt and #BAC6EA on deep blue. Yellow accents dark surfaces; blue action text on yellow. No yellow small text on pale backgrounds.

**Geometry/graphics:** convergence rather than enclosed nodes; generous continuous fields, humanist type and flowing alignment. Every diagram needs a labeled semantic job; the homepage motif relates contributing perspectives to one next question. No decorative radar, topography or glowing paths.

**Homepage:** centered first statement on desktop, dual actions and a small convergence explanation; collaborator and organization paths immediately follow, ahead of historical work. Research and evidence remain visible but the first impression prioritizes forming the lab.

**Mobile:** left-aligned headline for short scan lines; compact motif beside relevant copy; full-width audience paths. Menu and actions use the same accessible states as the other directions. Longer reading switches to the pale palette; no font-size reduction to preserve the desktop arrangement.

**Motion:** optional future single convergence transition after an explicit user action, never automatic endlessly moving lines. The current study is static. Reduced motion shows the same shared baseline without transition.

**Recruitment / customer fit:** strongest invitation to interdisciplinary collaborators; organization path is still equally visible. Risk: "shared problems" is less concrete than A's evidence framing; the next section must explain research/feasibility and source-linked work, not community-only aspirations.

**Applications:** one-color nested-path symbol; horizontal full name; vertical/compact versions; 16/24/32px; dark social/avatar, pale paper and blue digital product/documentation framing. A single family simplifies the loading budget; broad color fields require careful link/focus contrast.

**Tradeoffs:** cobalt/yellow contrast is strong in approved pairings but yellow-on-pale and muted-blue-on-blue need explicit prohibitions. Curves lose separation at tiny print/favicon sizes; consider a simplified optical master after selection, not a different unrelated symbol.

## Comparison and recommendation

These are design judgments, not measured audience preferences.

| Criterion | A — Open Register | B — Working Assembly | C — Common Ground |
|---|---|---|---|
| Research/evidence credibility | Strongest reading and record system | Strong but process-oriented | Strong if evidence follows invitation |
| Organization conversion clarity | Clear, measured | Strongest practical engagement framing | Clear but less delivery-oriented |
| Collaborator invitation | Serious, evidence-led | Tangible engineering emphasis | Strongest interdisciplinary invitation |
| Fit with honest formation stage | Natural with small real archive | Needs care not to imply capacity | Natural if it avoids invented community |
| Cross-industry flexibility | High | High, slightly industrial | High, slightly civic/network-like |
| Small mark / similarity risk | Generic brackets/frame risk | Generic assembly/chevron risk | Connectivity/bridge/arc risk |
| Implementation cost | Two type families; simple static layout | Static layout; condensed hierarchy care | One variable family; strict palette roles |

**Recommend A as the strategic starting point**, with the sans full-name wordmark for exploration. It lets Omniasec earn credibility through questions, methods and inspectable artifacts while visibly making room for engineering and commercial work. It also most naturally turns into the reusable research-record system the current site lacks. B is preferable if practical customer engineering should lead. C is preferable if recruiting interdisciplinary collaborators should dominate the opening impression.

Do not mix all three symbols, type systems and palettes. Selecting A does not finalize its symbol, typeface, tagline or name clearance; it authorizes a focused refinement round. A possible refinement is to give A's founder/formation and collaborator invitation the prominence of C, while retaining A's visual language.
