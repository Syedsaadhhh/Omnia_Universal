# Proposed palette roles

Measured with the WCAG sRGB relative-luminance formula; ratios in color-contrast.json. Test pairs, not isolated swatches. These calculations supplement browser axe and visual review; they do not establish whole-site accessibility.

A: pale text, sage accent and muted mineral on pine; deep green text on mineral; pale text on deep green buttons. B: charcoal/secondary brown on chalk; warm pale text on charcoal; white on oxide; charcoal on pale oxide. C: pale/blue-white/yellow on deep blue or cobalt; deep blue on pale/yellow; pale on cobalt buttons. Do not use sage, yellow or pale oxide as small text on light surfaces. Thin divider rules are decorative separators, not the only indicator of an interactive target.

All semantic body/action pairs in the JSON pass 4.5:1. Focus rings must be assessed against the adjacent surface and control separately in the final components. Actual 16/24/32px symbol recognition, optical spacing and print reproduction remain owner/design review items.
