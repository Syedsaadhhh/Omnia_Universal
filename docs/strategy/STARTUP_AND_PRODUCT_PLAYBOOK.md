# Omniasec Labs — research-to-product operating guide

Founder clarification, 18 October 2026: this reading supports our own strategy and decisions. It is not a public book collection or a reason to add features indiscriminately. Apply a principle to the website only when it improves a real visitor journey, evidence record or useful research workflow. The existing method, problem-intake prompts and blank record template are that limited application; no tracking, account system or customer database is implied.

Created 2026-10-17 in response to the founder's request to apply entrepreneurship/business learning. This is an initial curated library and original practical synthesis, not an exhaustive collection of major books or a claim that their complete texts were accessed. Research used authors' and publishers' public descriptions/articles linked below. No paid books were downloaded, copied, purchased or represented as fully read. Recommendations are adaptations to this founder-led lab, not independently validated success laws.

|---|---|---|---|
| **The Lean Startup — Eric Ries** | [Official principles](https://theleanstartup.com/principles) | Learning through a bounded experiment is progress; building more is not sufficient evidence. | Write a hypothesis and a continue/revise/stop rule before a prototype. These sources are useful but emphasize particular contexts, often software startups and commercial ventures. Some organizations need domain-specific regulation, long R&D cycles or public-interest measures. No source is treated as endorsement, a guaranteed playbook or a substitute for actual customer evidence. Richard Rumelt's official books listing was also checked, but it does not supply enough detail to claim a substantive extraction of **Good Strategy Bad Strategy** here; add a sourced strategy review later rather than inventing one.

## The lab's smallest useful operating system

Use plain documents and existing Git history first. No new runtime service or management platform is needed.

1. **Opportunity record:** who experiences the problem, a recent example, current workaround, consequences, alternatives, sources and access to relevant people. Label every inference.
2. **Assumption register:** importance, current evidence, what would falsify the assumption and the next cheap/safe test. Test the assumption that would most change the decision, not the feature easiest to build.
3. **Experiment record:** hypothesis; context/participants; permission/privacy/safety boundaries; budget; success and stop criteria defined beforehand; observations and counterevidence; decision, owner and review date.
4. **Team agreement:** required skills, actual availability, responsibilities, communication, attribution, ownership and any separately agreed commercial terms. Do not infer pay/equity from participation.
5. **Artifact record:** source/license, supported environment, dated tests, limitations, contribution history and current maintenance state. A demo is not automatically a supported product.
6. **Commercial decision:** only when relevant, examine buyer/user distinctions, willingness to make an actual commitment, distribution, support burden, unit economics, cash needs and legal/ownership issues. A venture is one possible outcome, not the required output of every investigation.

The public [blank record template](../../public/research-record-template.md) is deliberately limited to safe, non-confidential content. Sensitive interviews or customer information should not be committed to a public repository. Obtain consent before recording or publishing; minimize information collected. No new retention period or confidentiality agreement is asserted by this guide.

## Evidence gates mapped to the approved operating model

| Stage | Evidence to seek | Reason to pause or stop |
|---|---|---|
| Research | Relevant sources, known alternatives, gaps and uncertainty | The problem is already solved well enough or inaccessible to meaningful investigation. |
| Problem discovery | Recent observed events, affected people, current workaround and consequence | Only vague praise or founder enthusiasm; no identifiable workflow. |
| Validation | A predeclared test and an observation that changes a decision | Contradictory evidence, unusable sample, safety/privacy constraints or no meaningful result. |
| Team formation | Specific skills, availability and agreed responsibilities | No realistic owner, capacity or agreement about contribution/ownership. |
| Prototype engineering | Small artifact aimed at the uncertain mechanism | Scope grows without improving the test; unsafe dependence or unclear permission. |
| User testing | Relevant task outcomes and limitations, not only a demo working once | Real users cannot complete the task or the improvement is not meaningful. |
| Product development | Reliability, accessibility, privacy, support and sustainable delivery evidence | Maintenance/support obligations exceed capacity or value remains unsupported. |
| Potential venture | Market commitment, economics, team and ownership reviewed separately | Interest does not become a credible commitment, economics fail, or research is better shared another way. |

Do not impose arbitrary interview counts, conversion targets or revenue thresholds as universal gates. Define an appropriate threshold and its limitations for each decision. Strong evidence can still be wrong; keep counterevidence and revisit assumptions.

## Applied to this website and repository

- **Home:** clear positioning and honest formation stage; methods and genuine work precede any commercial aspiration. No fake metrics, logos or operational status.
- **For Organizations:** problem/workflow intake, existing attempts, constraints, desired change, feasibility before scope; no guaranteed capacity or result.
- **Collaborate:** relevant work, interests, availability and explicit agreement before commitments; no invented paid roles or equity offers.
- **Research and method:** distinguish observations, assumptions, experiments and findings; explicit continue/revise/stop logic; public blank record template.
- **Work:** type and readiness are separate; historical evidence and contribution links preserved; no promotion from hackathon artifact to commercial product by wording.
- **Updates:** date real engineering evidence and remaining risks. A green build is not a market test or proof of complete security.

Implemented pages make these principles useful without displaying a bookshelf as evidence of the lab's own achievements. Internal improvements can use these records immediately; actual interviews, outreach, customer commitments, payments, incorporation and funding decisions remain founder-controlled work, not actions performed by this session.
